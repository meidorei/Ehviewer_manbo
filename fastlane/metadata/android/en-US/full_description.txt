EhViewer manbo is an Android client fork based on EhViewer_CN_SXJ, with gallery browsing, search, favorites and downloads, plus local update tracking and reading management.

Features:
• Follow tags locally and import or export follow tags as JSON.
• Check saved searches across pages and mark shared unread gallery IDs as read across bookmarks.
• Run global Chinese scans or individual checks, with pause, resume and stop controls.
• Keep reading boundaries for follows and bookmarks, and a manual marker on the home list.
• Add Chinese search conditions automatically and look up JM metadata.
• Track recent reading and page progress for completed in-app downloads.
• Pause, resume and import new archive downloads.

Bookmark badges display 20+ above twenty unread entries while retaining all unread gallery IDs. Local follows retain up to 21 unread entries. Automatic deletion in the reading queue is off by default; enabling it permanently deletes local downloads evicted from the queue.

Local follows do not require server subscriptions. Site access still depends on network conditions and account permissions. See the repository README for project origins and documentation.
